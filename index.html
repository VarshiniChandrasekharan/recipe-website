Paneer Butter Masala

Ingredients:

  • Paneer - 200g (cubed)
  • Tomatoes - 3 (pureed)
  • Butter - 2 tbsp
  • Cream - 2 tbsp
  • Cashew nuts - 10 (soaked)
  • Spices: Garam masala, chili powder, turmeric, salt

Instructions:

  1. Grind soaked cashews into a paste.
  2. Heat butter, sauté tomato puree and spices.
  3. Add cashew paste, simmer and add paneer.
  4. Finish with cream and serve with naan or rice.
